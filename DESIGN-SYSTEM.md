# Design system
Aubergine #3A1F2B, Cloudy Blue #DFEDFF, Wet Cement #8E8E8E.
Derived dark neutral #5F5F5F supports readable small text.
Days One for wordmark and selected primary CTAs. Archivo for interface and headings. Playfair Display Italic as the explicitly approved licensed-font fallback.
Editorial hero uses supplied product photography. Product rail links resolve to shared catalogue identities. Keyboard focus, touch controls and reduced-motion support are required across routes.
